Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 2026

"Aeronef Academy Carousel Publisher" ("the Tool") is a personal, internal automation tool built and operated by the owner of the Aeronef Academy social media accounts.

What data the Tool handles

The Tool generates image and caption content locally on the operator's own computer and publishes it directly to social media accounts owned by the operator, using each platform's official API. It does not have user accounts, does not collect data from any third party, and does not share, sell, or transfer any data to any party other than the social media platforms the operator publishes to (TikTok, Instagram).

Third-party services

The Tool uses third-party APIs (TikTok Content Posting API, Instagram Graph API, and AI content-generation services) solely to publish the operator's own content. Each of those platforms' own privacy policies govern how they handle data sent to them.

No end-user data collection

Because this Tool is not distributed to or used by end users, it does not collect, store, or process personal data belonging to any person other than its operator.

Changes

This policy may be updated at any time as the Tool evolves.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the account owner via the Aeronef Academy social media channels.
